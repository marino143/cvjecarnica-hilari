Analytics with consent

Google Analytics 4 (G-8H40XGVSZZ) is enabled only after acceptance. Google receives connection data, including your IP address when connecting, browser and device information, cookie identifiers, the visited page, phone/WhatsApp click events and a successfully sent enquiry event. A click does not prove a completed call, message or sale. We use this data to understand visits and contact methods. We do not describe it as fully anonymous.

Google Ireland Limited provides Google Analytics; processing may involve Google LLC and transfers outside the EEA. Advertising, Google Signals and ad personalization are not enabled. Our manual events do not send form contents, names, email addresses, messages, WhatsApp queries or arbitrary URL query parameters and fragments. With consent, we retain only validated campaign labels (UTM), Google click identifiers and the referring origin without its path or query to measure visit sources. Campaign labels must not contain personal data.

Choice and withdrawal

Accept or reject in the privacy settings on every page. “Reject / withdraw consent” stops future analytics transmission and removes accessible _ga cookies. It does not delete data already sent to Google. You may grant consent again. If your choice cannot be saved, analytics stays off.

Your choice is stored locally under hilari.analytics.v2 until you change it or clear browser data. Analytics cookies _ga and _ga_* are configured for up to one year without automatic renewal; browser settings may shorten this. Retention within Google Analytics depends on the account settings.

Enquiries and external services

The contact form sends the entered data to Formspree to deliver your enquiry to the florist and enable a reply, not to Google Analytics. Do not enter sensitive information. WhatsApp, social media and Google Maps links open external services with their own privacy policies. The homepage loads an Instagram gallery through Behold.so, which receives connection information such as IP address and browser data when loaded; this is separate from Google Analytics consent. The map does not load automatically and fonts do not make requests to Google.
